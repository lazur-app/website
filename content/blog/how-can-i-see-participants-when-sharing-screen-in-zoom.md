To see participants when sharing your screen in Zoom on a Mac, you should enable Side-by-Side mode or manually show the Video Panel. These settings allow you to keep your audience visible while you present your content.

## Understanding the Zoom Screen Sharing Interface

When you start sharing your screen, the Zoom client often hides the participant video windows to give you more space for your slides or documents. This behavior is designed to help you focus on the content you are presenting. However, it can make it difficult to read the room. If you are presenting to a group, you might need to see facial expressions to gauge engagement. The default behavior is not always the best fit for every meeting type.

Most users find that the floating toolbar at the top of the screen is their primary way to manage this. When you are in a share, this bar contains all the controls you need. If you do not see it, move your mouse to the top of the screen. It should slide down into view. If it does not appear, you might need to ensure your Zoom window is in full-screen mode. This mode is the most reliable way to maintain access to the full suite of Zoom controls during a presentation.

## How to Enable Side-by-Side Mode

Side-by-Side mode is the most effective way to manage your screen space. It splits your display into two distinct areas. One area shows your shared content, and the other shows the participant video feeds. This layout prevents the video windows from overlapping your slides. It is a cleaner way to work than having floating windows scattered across your presentation.

To turn this on, follow these steps:

1. Start your screen share as you normally would.
2. Look for the floating toolbar at the top of your screen.
3. Click on the View Options menu.
4. Select Side-by-Side: Speaker or Side-by-Side: Gallery.

Once you select one of these options, your screen will adjust automatically. You will see a divider between the two sections. You can click and drag this divider to the left or right to change how much space each section takes up. If you have a wide monitor, this is particularly useful. You can give more space to the participants while keeping your slides readable.

## Using the Video Panel

If you prefer not to use Side-by-Side mode, you can use the Video Panel. This is a floating window that shows the participants. If this panel disappears during your presentation, you can bring it back easily. Move your mouse to the top of the screen to reveal the floating toolbar. Click the More (...) button and select Show Video Panel from the menu. This will bring the video window back to the front.

Within this panel, you have two main view options: Active Speaker and Gallery. Active Speaker shows a larger window for the person currently talking. This is helpful if you want to focus on the person delivering a point. Gallery View shows multiple participants in a grid. This is better for larger meetings where you want to see everyone at once. You can toggle between these by clicking the icons inside the video panel. You can also click and drag the panel to move it to a different part of your screen. If you prefer it at the bottom, just drag it there. The interface will snap the panel into place.

## Troubleshooting Common Visibility Issues

Sometimes, the settings do not work as expected. If you are struggling to see participants, check your Zoom settings. Go to the Zoom app and open Preferences. Look at the General tab. If you have the Use dual monitors option checked but are only using one screen, it can cause display issues. Try unchecking this box and restarting your share. This is a common fix for many display-related problems in Zoom.

Another point to consider is the version of your Zoom client. Zoom releases updates frequently. These updates often include changes to the interface and improvements to how panels behave. If you are using an older version, you might not see the same options as your colleagues. Always ensure you are running the latest version of the Zoom desktop client for the most stable experience. You can check for updates by clicking on your profile picture in the Zoom app and selecting Check for Updates.

## Managing Large Meetings

When you are presenting to a large group, seeing everyone is a challenge. Even with Side-by-Side mode, you might not be able to see every single person. In these cases, focus on the most active participants. If you use the Active Speaker view, Zoom will automatically highlight the person who is speaking. This is often enough to keep the conversation flowing. If you need to see more people, you can scroll through the participant list or use the Gallery view to see as many as your screen will allow. Remember that your primary goal is to share your content. Do not let the management of the video panel distract you from your presentation.

If you are a regular presenter, consider using a second monitor if your desk allows it. This is the ultimate solution for seeing participants. You can put your presentation on one screen and the participant video feed on the other. This gives you the best of both worlds. If you are using a laptop, you can connect an external display via HDMI or USB-C. Once connected, go to your Mac System Settings and ensure the displays are set up correctly. Then, in Zoom, you can enable the dual monitor feature in the settings menu. This will automatically split your Zoom interface across the two screens.
