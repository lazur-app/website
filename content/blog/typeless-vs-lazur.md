**Typeless vs Lazur** is relevant if you already believe dictation should polish natural speech, not dump a transcript, and you are shopping [Wispr Flow alternatives](/blog/wispr-flow-alternatives). Typeless is the “heavy everyday dictation, AI finishes the writing” slot on those lists. Lazur is the Mac [ambient writing](/blog/what-is-ambient-writing) slot: intent, context, Command Mode, local STT.

## Overlap and split

Both reject “raw speech on the page.” That is table stakes next to Wispr cleanup and Aqua’s high-quality text.

| | Typeless (as listed) | Lazur |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everyday polished dictation | Core pitch | Smart Rewrite |
| Intent / commands on current context | Confirm in-product | Command Mode |
| On-device STT | Confirm vendor docs | NVIDIA Parakeet on Mac |
| System-wide Mac | Confirm | Yes |
| Windows / mobile | Confirm | Windows coming; no mobile yet |

Always verify Typeless’s current platforms and privacy page, they move. Lazur’s public split is documented: [on-device STT](/blog/on-device-speech-to-text-mac), [pricing](/pricing).

## When Typeless is enough

You dictate all day, you want spoken language to look written, and you do not care about commanding “reply to this” or keeping audio on-device for transcription. Then Typeless (or Aqua, or Wispr) may already be the product.

## When Lazur is the better experiment

- The paste is pretty and you still rewrite the *job* (tone, reply, structure). [Messy speech](/blog/messy-speech-to-finished-writing).
- You want [Command Mode](/blog/command-mode-voice-writing).
- STT must be local. Superwhisper and [VoiceInk](/blog/voiceink-open-source-dictation) are the other privacy-shaped names.
