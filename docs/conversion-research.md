# Conversion research and site audit (Nov 2026)

Why: lazur.app gets traffic (mostly the ~2,600 SEO pages) but few visitors download or sign up.
Sources: transcripts of 15 YouTube videos on landing pages, CTAs, product-led growth, SaaS SEO and
PostHog, summarised below, plus a review of the live site on desktop and phone.

## What's wrong on lazur.app today

1. **Non-Mac visitors hit a dead end.** Phone and Windows visitors (a large share of search traffic)
   who click Download land on "Lazur is a Mac app" with only "Get notified for Windows". There's no way
   to send the Mac link to themselves, so the visit is lost. (Malewicz: give phones a realistic action.
   Cattoni: ~60% of buyers visit twice, so capture the first visit.)
2. **SEO pages have no call to action on the first screen.** Compare and how-to pages open with
   a headline, an intro paragraph and a summary box. The first CTA appears well down the page.
   (DAP: "it's not enough to rank". Diggity: comparison table and CTA near the top, CTA at the bottom.)
3. **No anxiety reducers next to Download.** The hero button says "Download for Mac. Free" with nothing
   under it about the trial, no credit card, Apple Silicon and Intel, or setup time. (McGlaughlin, Patel,
   Priestley: put 2–3 risk reducers right by the button.)
4. **No proof above the fold.** Testimonials and ratings exist further down the homepage, but none
   are near the hero. (Patel, HubSpot, Malewicz.)
5. **Abstract headline.** "Your AI companion, right at the notch or by your cursor" describes where
   Lazur sits, not what the visitor gets. Worth an A/B test against an outcome-first headline
   ("Get [result] without [problem]"). (Hormozi: ~90% of wins come from headline and hero tests.)
6. **No second touch.** Visitors who aren't ready to download leave nothing behind (no email,
   no reminder). (Cattoni, Alex/PMax: retarget non-converters.)

## Playbook (ranked by likely impact)

1. Win above the fold: headline, one visual and the main CTA visible without scrolling, on desktop and phone.
2. Headline = the visitor's outcome in the first words; proof in the sub-headline. Never open with "We…".
3. Give phone and Windows visitors an action they can take: "Email me the Mac link" (one field).
4. 2–3 anxiety reducers under every Download button: free trial, no credit card, Apple Silicon and Intel.
5. One primary action per page; secondary actions in a weaker (outline) style.
6. CTA copy: a verb, "free" when it's free, and what the visitor gets. Never "Learn more" after the pitch.
7. SEO pages: answer first, show Lazur doing the task, put a contextual CTA in the first screen, mid-article and at the end.
8. Social proof at decision points: hero, pricing, final CTA.
9. Handle the top objections (privacy, accuracy, works in every app) under the hero and again at the final CTA.
10. Plan for repeat visits: a low-commitment step for visitors who aren't ready.
11. Pop-ups only on events (scroll depth, time on page), never on page load.
12. Run one headline or hero A/B test per week and let the wins compound.
13. Hub-and-spoke internal links; no orphan SEO pages; prune pages with no visits and no conversions.
14. Product side: get to the first dictation within minutes, and email people who downloaded but never dictated.

## Video notes

- **Alex Hormozi, "3 Landing Page Tests"**: one split test a week, ~90% of them on the headline or the image under it; the gains come from above the fold.
- **Daniel Priestley, "The $1 Million Landing Page"**: hook (frustration or "are you ready to…"), value proposition, credibility, then a CTA that reduces risk ("free, 3 minutes, immediate results"). A quiz lead magnet gets 20–40% to start.
- **Neil Patel, "Anatomy of a High Converting Landing Page"**: ~6-word value-prop headline, a product visual, quantified benefits, one focused CTA, testimonials, a guarantee, storytelling. 8/10 people read the headline, 2/10 read the rest.
- **Alex Cattoni, "79% Conversion Rate"**: say what it is, why you'd want it and how to get it, all above the fold. ~60% of buyers viewed the page twice. Free offers deserve the same care as paid ones.
- **Matt Diggity, "CTA Masterclass"**: one CTA color used only for CTAs; button copy = verb + free + now + what you get. "Click here for lowest price" beat "Visit official website" by 85–283%. A recent "updated" date lifted clicks 14%. Always end with a CTA.
- **HubSpot, "Perfect Homepage"**: orient visitors in seconds, prioritise one conversion, put the step that predicts purchase (the demo) first, social proof at decision points, a sticky CTA plus a secondary one for people who aren't ready.
- **Copy That!, "$4.5M Landing Page"**: CuO headline (call out the reader, USP, objection) for warm traffic; feature blocks as name, benefit subhead, then benefit, benefit, outcome; rule of one (one reader, idea, emotion).
- **Malewicz, "Secret to Mobile Web Conversion"**: design mobile separately; 52–64px buttons; no squeezed screenshots or heavy animation; at most 2 form fields (email only); a CTA that's realistic on a phone.
- **Alex (Marketing for SaaS), "23% Conversion Rate"**: one keyword-rich sentence saying what the tool does; retargeting non-converters took conversion from ~1% to ~5%; a competitor-comparison page didn't convert for him, so measure compare pages separately.
- **Pedro Cortés, "PLG Landing Page Formula"**: tangible outcome, 5–10x better than the alternative, risk-free to try. Show the aha moment in the hero. "Get [result] without [problem]". Top 3 objections under the hero and again at the close.
- **ProductLed / Lucky Orange, "Five PLG Website Mistakes"**: 5–7 nav items; find the "effective fold" (where 50% stop) with scroll maps; value-adding CTA copy; event-triggered pop-ups only; name + email only on forms.
- **Olly Rosewell, "Should Your SaaS Have a Free Trial?"**: delayed-value products get ~40% higher conversion with trials; keep trial cost under ~20% of the price; make onboarding reach value fast, with progress bars and email nudges.
- **Flint McGlaughlin, "Optimizing Software Landing Pages"**: open the headline with the user's benefit and move proof to the sub-headline; put 2–3 anxiety reducers beside the download button; avoid parallel text columns.
- **Digital Authority Partners, "SaaS SEO"**: problem keywords convert better than "best tool" ones; solution pages beat generic how-tos; hub-and-spoke linking; pair SEO with CRO (trust, proof, next step on every page); prune weak pages.
- **PostHog, "Conversion funnels"**: funnel insights with renamed steps, breakdowns (path, UTM, device, country), strict vs sequential order, exclusion steps.
