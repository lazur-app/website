**Wispr Flow vs Aqua Voice vs Lazur** is the three-way most people actually mean when they search for a Wispr alternative. They are not three clones. They encode three theses:

- **Wispr Flow**, speak and I will type it for you.
- **Aqua Voice**, speak and I will turn it into high-quality text (fast, live, technical).
- **Lazur**, speak and I will understand the intent and finish the work.

The [Wispr Flow alternatives](/blog/wispr-flow-alternatives) list is longer (Superwhisper, Willow, VoiceInk, MacWhisper). This page is only the live-dictation race that looks like Wispr.

## Why this comparison exists now

“Voice → polished text” is becoming table stakes. Wispr has been moving toward command and context. Aqua has been moving toward live quality and voice editing. If Lazur positions as “Wispr, but with AI rewriting,” that moat shrinks.

The remaining product gap is messy human speech as a **spec**, not as a dirty transcript. See [messy speech to finished writing](/blog/messy-speech-to-finished-writing) and [ambient writing](/blog/what-is-ambient-writing).

## How to decide in one week

1. Same five messages: Slack, Gmail, a Cursor prompt, a “reply to this,” a jargon-heavy note.
2. Time start-to-send, including edits.
3. Note devices: if you need Windows or iPhone this month, Aqua or Wispr still have the footprint. Lazur is Mac-first.

[Download Lazur](/download) for the Mac third of that test. Keep Aqua or Wispr installed until the numbers are obvious.
