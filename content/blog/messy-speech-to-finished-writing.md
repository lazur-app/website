**Messy speech to finished writing** is the product demo that matters. People do not naturally speak like writers. They say:

> “Okay so, um, tell John that I looked at the thing and I think we should probably push it to next week because the numbers aren't ready yet, but don't make it sound like we're delaying it because of a problem.”

A transcription app hears a defect. [Voice writing](/blog/what-is-voice-writing) hears a spec: tell John, slip a week, numbers not ready, do not sound like a fire drill.

## The demo (copy this)

**You say**

> “uh can you tell Sarah that I looked at the deck and the second section needs another pass but overall it's looking good, keep it positive”

**Lazur pastes**

> Hey Sarah, I reviewed the deck. The second section could use another pass, but overall it’s looking really good.

Then you send. Almost no keyboard. That is the “zero editing” promise: *say it once; don’t edit it.* Not a vague “hours saved per week.”

Another one, Slack:

**You say**

> “hey Mike, can you move our meeting to Thursday afternoon, same agenda”

**Paste**

> Hey Mike, could we move our meeting to Thursday afternoon? Same agenda works for me.

Gmail: “reply and say I'll review it tomorrow, keep it warm.” Cursor: “refactor this to use zustand and add error handling for failed logins.” Same loop. [Intent Mode](/blog/command-mode-voice-writing) is when the speech is an instruction about *this* thread, not a draft of the reply.

## Why “sounding spoken” is not a user failure

Filler, asides, and “the thing” instead of the project name are how humans think out loud. Forcing people to speak like a prompt box is the old enemy: translating thoughts into computer instructions. The [ambient writing](/blog/what-is-ambient-writing) bet is the opposite.

Aqua and Wispr will keep getting better at pretty transcripts. If the paste is still the ramble with commas, you have not left transcription. See [Aqua Voice vs Lazur](/blog/aqua-voice-vs-lazur).

## How to practice this week

1. Do not clean up your speech on purpose.
2. Put the cursor in the real app, [emails by voice](/blog/how-to-write-emails-by-voice).
3. Include tone in the brief (“keep it positive,” “casual,” “not a problem”).
4. Score whether you still rewrite the paste.
