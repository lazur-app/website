You can use an AI voice over in CapCut Desktop by converting text layers directly into audio within the app interface. This built-in feature removes the need for external recording gear or third-party voice software during your editing process. 

## Setting Up Your AI Voice Over Workflow

CapCut Desktop has matured into a capable editor that handles generative audio natively. You no longer need to record your own voice or export files to other programs to get a professional sound. The core of this workflow relies on the Text-to-Speech engine. 

To start, open your project in the CapCut Desktop app. Navigate to the top toolbar and select the Text button. Drag a Default text layer onto your timeline. With this layer selected, move to the inspector panel on the right side of your screen. This is where you will input the script you want the AI to read. Type your text clearly to ensure the AI interprets the punctuation correctly. 

Once your text is ready, find the Text-to-Speech tab within that same right-hand panel. You will see a list of available voices. These are often sorted by language, gender, and emotional tone. Use the preview button next to each voice to audition how it sounds with your script. When you find one you like, click the Generate button. The app will process the request and place a new audio track on your timeline. This track is synced with your text layer, but it exists independently. You can now trim, move, or adjust the volume of the audio without affecting the text clip. You can even delete the original text layer if you only need the audio for your final video.

## Understanding the CapCut Pro Advantage

While the basic Text-to-Speech features are available to all users, the experience changes when you move to a Pro subscription. CapCut uses a system of AI points to manage usage of its more advanced generative tools. Pro subscribers typically receive a monthly allowance of 1,200 AI points. 

These points cover premium voice options and more complex tasks like voice cloning. Voice cloning allows you to create a synthetic version of a specific person's voice. This requires a clear sample of the target voice, which the AI analyzes to replicate the cadence and tone. Generating one minute of cloned voice usually costs between 10 and 25 AI points. 

It is important to check your current subscription status before you start a large project. Some features might be locked behind a paywall. If you add a premium asset, the export panel will alert you before you finish your project. Always verify your account status to avoid unexpected interruptions during the final export phase.

## Managing Audio Quality and Consistency

One common critique of built-in text-to-speech tools is that they can sound slightly robotic or flat. You can mitigate this by adjusting your script. Use punctuation like commas and periods to force the AI to pause naturally. Short, concise sentences often yield better results than long, complex ones. 

If you are creating a series of videos, consistency is key. Try to stick with the same voice profile across all your clips. This helps build a recognizable brand identity for your content. If you find that the default speed is too fast or slow, check the audio settings in the inspector panel. Small adjustments to pitch and speed can make a significant difference in how natural the voice sounds to your audience.

## Privacy and Ethical Considerations

Using AI to generate voices brings up important questions about consent and data. When you use voice cloning, ensure you have explicit permission to use the voice you are replicating. Never clone a voice without the owner's consent. 

CapCut updates its features frequently, so menu labels might shift over time. If you cannot find a button, look for it in the main inspector or the top toolbar. Relying on the current interface is always better than following an outdated tutorial. Keep your app updated to the latest version to ensure you have access to the most recent AI models and features.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

Many users struggle with syncing their audio to the video timeline. If your audio track feels off, remember that you can drag it independently. Do not rely solely on the auto-generated sync. Manual adjustments are often necessary for perfect timing. 

Another mistake is using too much text in a single block. If you have a long script, break it into smaller text layers. This makes it easier to manage the audio and allows you to change voices or tones for different sections of your video. This modular approach gives you more control over the final pacing of your edit. 

Finally, pay attention to the language settings. If you are producing content for a specific region, ensure the voice profile matches the dialect. CapCut offers a wide range of options, and choosing the right one can significantly improve the viewer's experience. 

## Integration with Auto Captions

AI voice overs are often paired with auto captions. CapCut's auto captioning tool is quite effective, with an accuracy range of 85 to 92 percent for clear English. After you generate your AI voice over, you can use the auto caption feature to generate text for the screen. 

This workflow is very efficient. The app typically takes 30 to 90 seconds to generate a full transcript and sync it to your timeline. Once the captions are generated, you can style them to match your video's aesthetic. This combination of AI voice and captions is a staple for modern short-form content. 

Keep in mind that while the AI is fast, it is not perfect. Always review the generated captions for errors. A quick manual check can save you from publishing content with typos. This two-part process—generating the audio and then the captions—is a powerful way to produce high-quality videos in a short amount of time.
