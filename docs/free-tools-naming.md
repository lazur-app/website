# Lazur free tools: subdomains and pages

49 free tools, each on its own subdomain. Drafted 2026-11-26.

## How this was made

- **Tool name = the phrase people type into Google.** The phrases come from Google's own suggestions and the titles of the pages ranking now (pulled with Serper on 2026-11-26).
- **Subdomain = that phrase, with hyphens.** Every tool gets its own subdomain. Nothing is folded into another tool.
- **Title** = the phrase first, then the extra words Google suggests people add ("free", "online", "no sign-up"), then `| Lazur`.
- **H1** = the phrase in plain words. The tagline under it says what the tool does.
- **Extra pages** = the same tool with its own title, H1 and intro, one page per longer search Google suggests (like "mic test with playback"). Only real suggestions are listed. Junk and off-topic suggestions were left out.
- **FAQ** = Google's "People also ask" questions for the phrase. Where Google showed none, the questions are drafted and marked *(drafted)*.
- **Every tool** links to `lazur.app/tools` and to 3-5 related tools, and ends with: "Want this in every app? Hold two keys and talk with Lazur."

Search numbers (monthly volume) are not checked yet. That needs a DataForSEO top-up.
