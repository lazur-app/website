Meta released **Muse for Mac** in September 2026, after Muse launched on mobile and the web. **Lazur** is an AI buddy that lives in your MacBook's notch. Both are AI assistants for the Mac, but they're built for different moments.

*We make Lazur. We've described Muse from Meta's announcement and press coverage; check Meta's site for current features and availability.*

## The core difference

**Muse is a task agent.** You hand it a job, like organising a folder, filling a form from your files, or summarising your day from email, messages and notes, and it works across your files and native apps to do it, asking for approval before sensitive actions.

**Lazur is a buddy at your side.** It's for the moments in the middle of your work: "what does this error mean?", "where's the share button?", "how do I change my email here?", "remind me at 5", "reply that Thursday works". You hold a key, ask, and it answers out loud, points at the exact spot, or does it.

## When Lazur is the better fit

- **You're learning software.** New design tool, new admin panel, a setting buried three menus deep. Lazur shows you where, one click at a time.
- **You switch apps a hundred times a day.** Asking without leaving the window keeps you in flow (see [why context switching costs so much](/blog/stop-context-switching-ai-buddy)).
- **You type a lot.** Hold fn and talk; clean text lands at your cursor.

## When Muse is the better fit

- You want an agent to work through your personal data on its own.
- You already use Muse on your phone and want continuity.

## Privacy

Lazur listens and looks only while you hold its shortcut; audio and screenshots aren't stored, and nothing you do trains models ([privacy policy](/privacy)). Muse asks for explicit approval before sensitive actions; read Meta's terms for what it keeps.
