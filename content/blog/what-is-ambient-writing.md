**Ambient writing** is a voice interface for work. You do not open an app called the writer. You stay in Slack, Gmail, or Cursor. You press one hotkey, speak the messy version, and the useful version appears at the cursor. Lazur uses this name on purpose: it should feel like a capability the computer has, not a tool you launch.

The promise is not “AI voice dictation for Mac.” Everyone will have good STT and cleanup. The promise is: **you do not have to formulate your thoughts for the computer.**

## Category vs adjacent products

| Label | What it usually means |
| --- | --- |
| Dictation / STT | Speak → words |
| [Voice writing](/blog/what-is-voice-writing) | Speak → send-ready text |
| Ambient writing | Speak → result, *without leaving the current app or opening a chat* |
| Voice to action | Speak → command ([Command Mode](/blog/command-mode-voice-writing)) |

Wispr is “speak and I'll type it.” Aqua is “speak and I'll make high-quality text.” Superwhisper is private control of processing. VoiceInk is own the stack. Lazur’s thesis sits here: *Work by voice. Not by prompts.*, closer to voice → contextual AI → action than voice → transcript.

## What ambient is not

- A window you paste threads into.
- A per-app plugin.
- A developer-only identity. The audience is anyone whose job is turning thoughts into text: founders, engineers, designers, sales, operators. Cursor is a beachhead, not the whole market.

Privacy is proof, not the headline. On-device speech recognition, no voice recordings stored for STT, no training on your dictation for generalized models. Copy: *your thoughts stay yours*, then the bullets. [Privacy](/privacy), [on-device STT](/blog/on-device-speech-to-text-mac).

## The loop

Press. Speak. Continue working.

That is why system-wide paste matters more than a beautiful editor. [How to use dictation on a Mac](/blog/how-to-dictate-on-mac). [Messy speech](/blog/messy-speech-to-finished-writing) is the content that belongs in the demo.

Wispr’s own arc (reliable input → voice to action → ubiquity) is walking toward this category. Do not position Lazur as a rewrite skin on Wispr. Position it as: **the computer shouldn’t make you speak like a computer.**
