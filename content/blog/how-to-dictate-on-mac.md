How to use dictation on a Mac: click into a text field, start dictation, and speak. That is live **speech to text** and **voice typing** at the cursor, not a converter website that uploads audio. Apple includes a free option. If you write all day in Slack, Gmail, Notion, and Cursor, a system-wide AI app such as [Lazur](/download) is the more reliable path. It pastes finished text wherever you type.

## Turn on Apple dictation

1. Open **System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation**.
2. Turn **Dictation** on.
3. Pick a shortcut (double-press Control, or the microphone key).
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone** and allow the apps that will listen.

That is enough for Notes, Mail, and Messages. For the privacy split between local and cloud, see [on-device speech to text on Mac](/blog/on-device-speech-to-text-mac).

## Apple dictation vs an AI dictation app

**Apple dictation** is free, on-device on recent macOS versions, and fine for short notes. It is the “speech to text free” answer if you stay inside Apple apps.

**AI dictation apps** add rewrite, a personal dictionary, and one hotkey that works in every window. That is [voice writing](/blog/what-is-voice-writing), not just a transcript.

If you are choosing between the two, read [Apple dictation vs AI dictation](/blog/apple-dictation-vs-ai-dictation).

## Step-by-step: use dictation on macOS

1. **Microphone.** System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. Enable your dictation app (and Accessibility, if the app pastes system-wide).
2. **Cursor.** Click into the field you will send from, not a scratch pad you will copy later.
3. **Hotkey.** Start Apple dictation, or hold your AI app’s shortcut.
4. **Speak in thoughts, not syllables.** “Follow up with Priya tomorrow about the invoice” beats “follow… up… with…”
5. **Stop and scan.** Read the paste once. Fix names. Send.

That loop is how you [write emails by voice](/blog/how-to-write-emails-by-voice) without sounding like a transcript.

## Tips that actually improve accuracy

- Use a decent mic. AirPods work; a desk mic is better in a noisy office.
- Add product names and teammates to a personal dictionary when the app supports it.
- Pause between ideas so punctuation can land.
- Dictate the *intent* (“polite decline, offer next Tuesday”) and let rewrite handle the prose. That is where Lazur’s Smart Rewrite earns the setup.

## Common mistakes

**Dictating into Notes, then copying.** You add friction. Paste at the cursor in the real app.

**Whispering.** Speak at conversation volume. Whispered audio is harder for every STT engine.
