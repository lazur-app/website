If dictation on Mac is not working, the issue is typically a configuration mismatch or a software conflict rather than a hardware failure. Follow these steps in order to resolve the most common problems.

## Quick Fixes for Dictation Issues

Start by toggling the service. Go to System Settings > Keyboard. Find the Dictation section. Turn it off. Wait 10 seconds. Turn it back on. This simple reset forces the Mac to reconnect to its speech recognition resources. It is the most common fix for temporary glitches.

Next, restart your Mac. A full reboot clears stuck background processes. Sometimes the sound engine hangs, and a restart is the only way to re-initialize it. If that fails, check for software updates. Go to System Settings > General > Software Update. Apple often releases patches for system-level features in point updates. You might be running a version that has a known bug.

## Checking Your Microphone Input

Your Mac cannot transcribe what it cannot hear. Go to System Settings > Sound > Input. Select your active microphone. Speak into it. If the input level bar does not move, your Mac is not receiving audio. Check your physical connections if you use an external mic. If you use the built-in mic, ensure it is not obstructed by a case or dust. 

Verify app-level permissions. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Ensure the application you are using has permission to access your microphone. If the toggle is off, the app will remain silent during dictation attempts.

## The Conflict with Voice Control

Many users do not realize that standard Dictation and Voice Control cannot run at the same time. They compete for the same audio stream. If you have Voice Control enabled in System Settings > Accessibility, turn it off. Test your dictation again. You will often find it works immediately after disabling the accessibility feature.

## Language and Region Settings

In System Settings > Keyboard, click Edit next to Languages. Ensure the language you are speaking is selected. If you see a download icon, click it. You need the language pack installed for local processing. If your internet connection is unstable, the download might have stalled. Connect to a stable network and retry the download.

## Understanding the 30-Second Rule

Apple's native dictation is designed to stop automatically after 30 seconds of silence. This is not a bug. It is a feature to save battery and processing power. If it stops while you are speaking, your Mac may be struggling to detect your voice. This happens in noisy environments. Try moving to a quieter space or bringing the microphone closer to your mouth.

## When to Use a Dedicated Voice Assistant

Native dictation is fine for simple notes, but it lacks advanced features. It does not handle complex formatting or custom snippets well. If you find yourself frustrated with the limitations of the built-in tool, you might need a more capable assistant.

## Advanced Troubleshooting for macOS 27

If you have tried all the above and dictation still fails, check the system logs. Open Console.app and search for speech recognition errors. This can reveal if a specific daemon is crashing. For those on Apple silicon, ensure you have at least 12GB of unified memory. The system needs this overhead to process speech on-device. Without it, the Mac may fall back to cloud processing or fail to trigger entirely.

## Common User Mistakes

Do not assume the shortcut is working. Go to System Settings > Keyboard and look at the Shortcut pop-up. Are you pressing the right keys? Many users change this to a custom key and forget. Try triggering it manually via the Edit menu in your app. If Edit > Start Dictation works, your shortcut is the problem. Remap it to something easier to remember or reach.

## Summary of Hardware Needs

| Requirement | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |

| Chipset | M3 or later for on-device |
| Network | Stable connection for language packs |
| Permissions | Microphone access in Privacy settings |

By following these steps, you should restore your voice typing capabilities. If the problem persists, ensure your OS is fully updated to the latest version of macOS 27.
