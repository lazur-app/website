An **AI work assistant**, a dictation app, and ChatGPT solve different problems. Dictation converts speech into words. ChatGPT gives you a general-purpose conversation. A context-aware AI work assistant uses the workflow already in front of you to help produce the finished result.

Lazur sits in the third category while still supporting voice input and polished writing on macOS.

## At a glance

| Tool | Best at | Main limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dictation app | Converting speech to text | It may leave structure and decisions to you |
| ChatGPT | Research, brainstorming, and open-ended questions | You usually move context into a separate chat |
| AI work assistant | Completing context-heavy tasks in your current app | It still needs a clear goal and human review |

## Dictation apps

Dictation is the right tool when you know what you want to say. It can be fast and private, especially when speech-to-text runs locally. But a transcript is not always a finished message. You may still need to organize the thought, choose a tone, and connect it to the thread you are answering.

## ChatGPT

ChatGPT is powerful when the task benefits from a conversation. It can explain, brainstorm, compare, and reason across a large prompt. The tradeoff is workflow friction: you may need to copy the email, paste the code, describe the selection, and move the result back to your app.

## AI work assistants

A context-aware work assistant is optimized for the last mile of work:

- “Reply to this and keep it concise.”
- “Rewrite this for a customer.”
- “Turn the selected code into an agent task.”
- “Summarize this thread and list the decision.”

## Which should you choose?

Choose dictation for exact speech-to-text, ChatGPT for a deliberate AI conversation, and Lazur when you want to stay inside Slack, Gmail, Cursor, Notion, or another Mac app while moving from thought to finished work.
