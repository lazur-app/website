An **AI assistant for email replies** uses the existing thread to help you write the message you mean to send. Instead of dictating the reply sentence by sentence, you can say the outcome: “Reply that Thursday works, ask for the deck, and keep it warm.”

Lazur is a voice-first AI email assistant for Mac. It helps turn a short request and the email context already open into a ready-to-review response.

## What to include in an email request

A strong request usually has four parts:

- **Intent:** reply, follow up, clarify, decline, or thank
- **Facts:** dates, decisions, status, or requested next steps
- **Tone:** warm, direct, concise, formal, or casual
- **Constraints:** length, audience, or anything not to promise

You do not need to repeat the subject line or recipient if the assistant can use the active thread.

## Examples

| Situation | Request |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduling | “Reply yes, Thursday afternoon works, keep it warm.” |
| Follow-up | “Follow up on this and ask for an update by Friday.” |
| Tone change | “Make this more direct without sounding annoyed.” |
| Long thread | “Summarize the decision and draft the next reply.” |

## Email privacy and review

Email is sensitive. Use an assistant with clear controls for app context, screen permissions, storage, and cloud processing. Review every generated message before sending; a polished draft can still contain an incorrect assumption.

## Bottom line

The best AI email assistant reduces context switching while keeping the human in control. Open the thread, state the outcome, review the draft, and send only when it is accurate.
