Studying is full of small stuck moments: a diagram you can't parse, a paragraph that reads like fog, a formula with one symbol you don't recognise. Each one tempts you to open a chat, paste it in, and lose twenty minutes somewhere else.

An **AI study buddy** helps right where the confusion is.

## Explanations in context

With Lazur on your Mac, hold ⌥ ⌘ and ask about whatever's on screen:

- "Explain this diagram simply."
- "What does this paragraph actually mean?"
- "What's this symbol in the second equation?"
- "Give me an example of this concept."

Lazur sees the page and **answers out loud**, so your eyes stay on the material. It can also **point** at the part of a diagram it's talking about.

## Turn messy notes into clean ones

Hold fn and talk through what you just learned, as messily as you like: "okay so the main causes were first the trade routes second the new taxes and third the drought". Lazur types it as a clean, numbered list in your notes app.

## Stay on top of everything else

- "Remind me the essay is due Friday."
- "Timer for 50 minutes, study", which ticks in your notch so you can see it.
- "Search for sources on this."

## Use it to learn, not to skip learning

The best way to use an AI study buddy is the way you'd use a patient tutor: ask why, ask for another example, ask it to check your explanation. Asking it to explain beats asking it to answer.

Your school may have rules about AI in graded work. Follow them.
